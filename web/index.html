About

Reflex Reimagined is a web remake of REFLEX v1.2.3, a 1988 EGA shareware game by 3F Productions of Chicago. It was built by reverse-engineering the original REFLEX.EXE (Turbo C, 1987).

Every sprite is the original artwork, decoded straight from REFLEX.EGA. The game rules, level progression, timing and sounds all follow the original machine code, including the parts the 1988 manual never mentioned. The biggest of those: the board evolves. Each turn, random tiles move one step along a fixed chain, from trail to empty space to Pigman and on up to Barren.

What's new: the look, smooth animation, particles, a dark palette (switch to Classic EGA in Settings), gamepad and touch controls, and a Top Forty saved in your browser.

"REFLEX is distributed on a shareware basis. Try this game out, if you like it please send us $15." 3F Productions, 1988

REFLEX and its artwork belong to their original authors. This is a non-commercial tribute.
